Subject: Request: set up automatic recording of Frontier's hourly actuals, as planned for Grays Harbor

Hello,

We're asking BPA to set up automatic recording of hourly actual generation for Frontier, and for GCPUD if it is still in the hourly check-out. Background (for internal readers and anyone who needs the detail)

How the check-out works today
- At the top of every hour, the trader on shift calls the generator (Frontier, and Grays Harbor until BPA's automatic system covers it) for its ACTUAL output, not an estimate.
- The trader calls BPA and reads in the actual figure. In June this hourly BPA check-out also covered GCPUD, with the call made between 25 and 35 minutes after the hour, right after the EPE dispatcher call.
- The trader initials the hour in the services book.
- The current instruction says this must be done even when the generator is prescheduled, and must continue "until these generators are automatically metered." So under the instruction already in force, automatic metering is the point where the calls stop.

Why this matters
- The calls compete with trading and have often been missed.
- BPA's contact said they were inclined to recommend to BPA that Enron is incapable of providing this service because of inconsistent performance.

What changes after metering
- Real-time traders stop the hourly generator call, the BPA call and the initialling.
- The desk steps in only if the meter feed fails. Before go-live we will agree with BPA how a failed feed is flagged, and the trader then goes back to the manual calls for those hours.

Estimated savings to the desk
- About 3 minutes per hour for Frontier alone. The instruction expects the whole process, when it covered two generators plus BPA, to take under 5 minutes.
- 24 times a day, which is about 730 a month, comes to about 36.5 trader-hours a month, or about $3,102.50 a month.
- Grays Harbor is not counted in the savings, because BPA was already automating it and it stops being a customer on Oct. 1.

Open points to confirm
- Whether GCPUD is still covered by the hourly check-out. If it is, include it in the request.
- Who at Frontier can provide meter or telemetry access.
