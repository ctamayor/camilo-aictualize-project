- "In the Enpower Path Confirmation, we need to make cuts as Path Confirms. Enter comments in the confirmed section." Each cut adds another save.

How it would work after the change:
- The trader highlights a counterparty's paths, clicks the telephone icon, accepts the pre-filled contact (or edits it), and moves straight on to the next call.
- The phone calls stay the same. Only the typing and the wait for the save go away.

Things to confirm while you design it:
